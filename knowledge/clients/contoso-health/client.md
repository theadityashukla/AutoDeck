# Contoso Health

> **Fictional.** Contoso is a long-standing placeholder company name in sample datasets.
> Nothing here describes a real organisation.

## Who they are

A regional healthcare provider evaluating a clinical-documentation assistant. They draw on
the shared project corpus — inference efficiency is inference efficiency — and share
nothing else with any other engagement: different regulatory position, different latency
profile, different audience.

That split is the two-tier structure (D8) doing its job. The evidence is reusable; the
context is not.

## What they care about

1. **Clinical safety.** An unacceptable answer is a patient-safety issue, not a support
   cost. This inverts the usual ordering — quality tolerance leads and cost follows.
2. **Auditability.** They need to show a regulator where an assertion came from. The
   citation chain is the product here, not a nicety.
3. **Cost.** Real, but third.

## Deliberately thin

Two required files and a short profile. This folder exists so client isolation has a second
real namespace to operate on rather than only a test fixture (see `knowledge/README.md`);
it is not a worked engagement, and padding it with invented detail would make the seed
corpus look like it holds more evidence than it does.

## Accuracy note

Engagement context, not evidence. Nothing here is citable.
