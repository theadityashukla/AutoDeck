# A deck to build like

The person asked for this deck to be built **like** one of the client's past decks. Below
it is described by what code measured in it: the storyline (each slide's role and title,
in order, and where sections break), how its headers are written, its voice, and how dense
its slides are.

Use it for **shape and voice**:
- the arc: how it opens, how many sections, where the evidence sits, how it closes and asks;
- the rhythm: how dense slides are, how often a divider or a single big point appears;
- the header voice: length, case, assertion or topic, whether numbers lead.

It is **never a source**. Its titles and words are not evidence and are not in the
evidence pack: nothing from it may appear on a slide as a fact, a number or a name unless a
passage you were shown says it, cited as usual. A past deck that said something is not proof
it was ever right. Do not copy its titles; write this deck's own, in the same voice, about
this deck's evidence. Where this deck's evidence or the person's request calls for a
different shape (more slides, no appendix, a different order), the request and the evidence
win, and you say so in your notes.
