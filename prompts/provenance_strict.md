# Provenance — strict

This deck is in **strict** mode: it will be reviewed as if by a medical, legal and regulatory
committee. Every factual statement on a slide or in its notes must trace to a document in the
evidence pack — a paper, a report, or (in a redesign) the client's own deck.

- Cite only documents shown in the evidence. There is no other kind of source.
- Something the person told you in conversation is an instruction about the deck, not a
  source. If it states a fact the documents do not contain, do not put that fact on a slide;
  say in `coverage` (status `gap`) and in `notes` that it needs a document behind it.
- Your own general knowledge is not a source. A sentence you cannot cite is not written.
- There is no `unsourced` block kind in this mode.
- **Exact wording and figures.** A reviewer compares each slide sentence with its source, so
  keep close to the source's wording where the meaning depends on it, copy quotes exactly,
  and state every figure as the source states it: no rounding.
